Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Nova Fit AI ("we," "us," or "our") operates the @iamnova.fit TikTok creator account and the novafitai.com website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our services and our use of the TikTok API.

1. Who We Are

Nova Fit AI is an AI-powered fitness content creator account on TikTok. We use automated tools to produce and publish daily fitness content — workout tips, nutrition advice, and mindset content — to the @iamnova.fit TikTok account. This application is operated by Ryan Caruso (ryanpaulcaruso@gmail.com).

2. Information We Collect

We collect very limited information:

  • TikTok account credentials — when authorizing our posting tool via TikTok Login Kit, we receive an access token for the @iamnova.fit creator account. This is used solely to publish content to that account.
  • Contact information — if you email us directly, we receive your name and email address.
  • Website usage data — standard server logs including IP address, browser type, and pages visited on this website.

We do not collect any data from TikTok viewers, followers, or third-party users. We do not access or store data from any TikTok account other than @iamnova.fit.

3. TikTok API Data

Our application uses TikTok's Content Posting API and Login Kit for the following limited purposes:

  • video.upload / video.publish — to upload pre-produced fitness videos to the @iamnova.fit account on an automated schedule.
  • user.info.basic — to verify the identity of the authorized creator account before posting.

TikTok API access tokens are stored securely and used exclusively to post content to the authorized account. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond publishing content. We do not share TikTok account data with any third party.

You may revoke our access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's app settings under Settings → Privacy → Apps and Websites.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Publish AI-generated fitness content to the @iamnova.fit TikTok account
  • Verify the authorized account prior to posting
  • Respond to direct inquiries sent to us via email
  • Maintain and improve this website

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, except:

  • Service providers — AI generation services (Higgsfield, ElevenLabs, Creatomate) receive content briefs but not your personal data. They do not receive TikTok credentials.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention

TikTok access tokens are stored only as long as needed to maintain the posting schedule. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us or revoking access via TikTok's settings. Contact information (email) is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect stored credentials and access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw TikTok account authorization at any time via TikTok app settings

10. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with TikTok's platform. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We are not responsible for TikTok's data practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at ryanpaulcaruso@gmail.com.
